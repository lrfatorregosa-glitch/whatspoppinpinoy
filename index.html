      Mula sa mga tagapangasiwa

      A note from the curators, how we made this museum, and every source and photo credit.

      Bakit ang dekada 2000?

      We chose 2000 to 2010 because it is the decade when Filipinos first started living part of their lives online. Texting, internet cafes and Friendster made the Philippines the "texting capital of the world," and many of today's habits, from group chats to online fundraising, started there.

      Our museum follows one question through six areas of Filipino life: social, economic, political, cultural, environmental, and OFW and diaspora life. For each one, we compared the 2000s with today, then used that pattern to predict what Philippine pop culture might look like in 2030.

      The 2030 floor shows our own predictions, not facts. We hope this museum makes you look at your phone, your playlist and your barkada a little differently.

      — Ang mga tagapangasiwa
