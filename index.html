Paano gumalaw · How to move

Keyboard and mouse: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, Shift to run. Click anywhere in the room to look around with the mouse; press E or click when the dot is on an exhibit or door. Esc frees the cursor.
Phone or touch: use the joystick at the bottom left to walk (push it all the way to run), drag the screen to look around, and tap a white circle to view an exhibit.
